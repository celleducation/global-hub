Detailed Program

Agenda for the day

A compact overview of the full invitation-only experience: 09:00-13:00 at Estadi Mallorca Son Moix / RCD Mallorca, then from 15:00 tennis, cocktails, dinner and public viewing at Mallorca Country Club.

Estadi Mallorca Son Moix · RCD Mallorca09:00-13:00
09:00
Welcome and opening remarks
Short welcome by the host, framing of the Longevity Lab and introduction to the guests, venues and flow of the day.
09:15
Mallorca management welcome
Official welcome by the local management team and orientation for the morning program at Estadi Mallorca Son Moix.
09:30
Dr. Kay Bredehorst · Cell Performance
Keynote part 1 on the biological foundations of cell performance, therapeutic relevance, patient communication and the role of cellular health in longevity medicine. Scheduled until 11:00.
11:00
Catering break
Thirty-minute break with catering, refreshments and time for first conversations between doctors, partners and invited members.
12:00
Dr. Kay Bredehorst · Global Hub for Cell Performance
Keynote part 2 with the Global Hub concept, network structure and partner presentation videos. Each partner video is planned as a concise 1-2 minute introduction.
13:00
Catering, discussion and closing exchange
Relaxed closing of the morning session with catering, open discussion, questions and direct exchange around next steps in the network.
13:30
Free departure
Individual transfer to the hotel, into Palma or directly onward to the afternoon program.
Mallorca Country Club15:00+

Country Club tennis tournament

17:30
Cocktail with finger food

18:30
Dinner
Shared dinner until 20:30 with curated table conversations, doctors, selected partners and invited guests.

Public viewing · Germany vs Ivory Coast
Shared World Cup public viewing as the evening finale: Germany against Ivory Coast, with drinks and informal closing conversations.

Morning seminar

The morning is designed as a concentrated scientific and strategic session around cell performance, longevity and the Global Hub ecosystem.

  • Opening remarks and official welcome
  • Two-part lecture by Dr. Kay Bredehorst
  • Partner videos and moderated exchange

Afternoon and evening

The second part of the day creates space for relationship-building in a premium sports and hospitality environment.

  • Country Club tennis tournament
  • Cocktail, finger food and dinner
  • Evening speech and public viewing

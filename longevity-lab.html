Longevity Lab Mallorca 2026

A focused day dedicated to the future of cellular medicine, longevity and human performance. At Estadi Mallorca Son Moix and Mallorca Country Club, the Global Hub brought together medicine, science, elite sport, entrepreneurship and innovation in one shared conversation.

What Happened

Science, sport and medicine in one room.

Longevity Lab Mallorca 2026 connected physicians, scientists, athletes, entrepreneurs, investors and innovators around one question: how can better cellular science improve healthspan, recovery and human performance?

The event opened at Estadi Mallorca Son Moix, hosted in the setting of RCD Mallorca. The morning program focused on cellular medicine, bionic cell therapy, performance physiology and the structure of the Global Hub for Cell Performance.

A central theme was the bridge between elite sport and longevity medicine: how cellular function, energy production and recovery influence sustained physical and mental performance under pressure.

The day continued at Mallorca Country Club with ATP tennis, informal exchange and an evening program that connected medical insight with sport, entrepreneurship and international collaboration.

Network

A European network around healthspan and performance.

Longevity Lab Mallorca 2026 brought together voices from medicine, science, sport, pharmacy, clinics, investment and entrepreneurship. Contributors and guests included representatives from RCD Mallorca, Mallorca Country Club, Burg Apotheke, NADclinic, ONE Life Sciences, Papimi, Alpstein Clinic and the broader Global Hub ecosystem.

The event featured exchange with personalities from elite sport including Roman Weidenfeller, Gabriela Sabatini, Denise Höfer and Adam Progress, alongside physicians, scientists, therapists, coaches, entrepreneurs and partners from across Europe.

The core idea behind the day was clear: longevity is not one discipline, one technology or one company. It is built through collaboration between science, medicine, elite sport, entrepreneurship and education.

The future of longevity is built together.

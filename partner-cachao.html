Why We Chose This Partner

Clean cacao with a premium nutrition profile.

The Global Hub highlights partners that bring clarity, quality and a credible fit to cellular performance, longevity and everyday health routines. Cachao was selected because its cacao products are simple, traceable and close to the original ingredient.

01

Organic Criollo Cacao

Cachao works with organic single-origin Criollo cacao, a premium cacao variety known for its distinct aroma and naturally rich profile.

02

Minimal Processing

The products are processed with restraint, helping preserve the natural character of the cacao bean without unnecessary industrial steps.

03

No Additives

The selected cacao products are built around ingredient purity: no added sugar, no unnecessary fillers and no complicated positioning.

04

Everyday Performance Fit

Cacao integrates easily into breakfast, smoothies, functional recipes and routines for people interested in nutrition quality.
